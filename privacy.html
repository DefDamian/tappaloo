Tappaloo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026
Developer: Damian Cedeno ("Developer," "we," "us," or "our")
Contact: dca.support@icloud.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tappaloo mobile application (the "App") handles information. The App is offered free of charge and only in the United States. By downloading, installing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect, store, sell, share or transmit any personal information.
  • The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.
  • Everything you enter or create in the App, including the optional name you type, your scores, streaks, stars, trophies, pictures and settings, stays on your device, unless you choose to turn on the optional Apple features described in Section 3.
  • The App works fully offline.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following data locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage:

Data Purpose Leaves your device?
The name or nickname you choose (optional) Shown on the Play screen as a greeting No
Game results (score, date, taps, mistakes) Statistics, streaks and daily goals No
Preferences (theme, sound, music, haptics, difficulty, hidden games, reminder time, accessibility options) Remembering your settings No
Stars, trophies (achievements), weekly missions and Reward Shop items Rewards and progress No
Finished Color by Math pictures (Art Gallery) Letting you view and share your artwork No (unless you share one yourself)
A small summary for the Home Screen widget (streak, today's challenge, stars) Showing the widget No. It is shared only between Tappaloo and its own widget on your device, using an Apple "App Group"

We never receive this data and cannot see, access or recover it. You may enter a nickname instead of your real name, or skip the name entirely.

Device backups. If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, Apple's operating system may include the App's on-device data in your device backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us, and we have no access to them.

3. Optional Apple features (off by default)

These features are off until you turn them on in Settings → Game Center & iCloud. They are provided by Apple, and the Developer does not receive your data through them.

  • Apple Game Center. If you turn it on and sign in, your game scores and achievements are sent to Apple Game Center and shown on leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) governs Game Center. You can turn it off at any time in the App or in iOS Settings → Game Center.
  • iCloud sync. If you turn it on, your game history is stored in your own private iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage) so it can sync between your devices. The Developer cannot access your iCloud account or this data.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and widgets. Siri phrases such as "Play the daily challenge in Tappaloo" are handled by Apple on your device. Widgets read only the summary described above.
  • Sharing. When you tap "Share Score" or share a picture, iOS opens the standard share sheet, and you choose where it goes. Nothing is shared without your action.

4. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the App asks for permission to send local notifications. These are scheduled on your device by iOS. No notification data is sent to us or any server. You can turn reminders off at any time in the App (Settings → Reminders) or in iOS Settings.

5. Information we do not collect

We do not collect: names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone data, health data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers (IDFA), IP addresses, usage analytics, crash analytics, or purchase information. The App does not use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies.

6. Information you send us

If you choose to contact us by email (for example at dca.support@icloud.com), we will receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to respond to you. We do not add you to mailing lists or share your email. We keep support emails only as long as reasonably needed to help you, then delete them.

7. Apple

The App is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may collect information about your download and use of the App store under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive personal information from Apple about you. If you choose to share anonymous diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, non-identifying crash reports; we use these only to fix bugs.

8. Children's privacy (COPPA)

The App is suitable for a general audience, including children. Game Center and iCloud sync are off by default and are controlled by Apple's own parental controls and account settings. Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, it does not collect personal information from children as defined by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The optional name field is stored only on the device and is never transmitted to us. Parents or guardians may delete all App data at any time by deleting the App or using Settings → Statistics Data → Reset in the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your privacy rights (including California and other U.S. state laws)

Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and other U.S. states with consumer privacy laws may have rights to know, access, correct, delete or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not collect it in the first place, so there is no personal information for us to provide, correct or delete, apart from any support emails you send us. To ask about or delete a support email, contact dca.support@icloud.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.

"Do Not Track." Because we do not track users across apps or websites, the App does not respond to Do Not Track signals; there is nothing to opt out of.

10. Data security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's built-in security (such as your passcode and Apple's data protection). We encourage you to keep your device and iOS up to date.

11. Deleting your data

You can delete all App data at any time by:
1. using Settings → Statistics Data → Reset Everything in the App (this also clears stars, trophies and your Art Gallery), or
2. deleting the App from your device.

If you turned on iCloud sync, you can also remove synced data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. If you used Game Center, you can manage Game Center data in iOS Settings → Game Center.

12. United States only

The App is intended only for users located in the United States and is distributed only on the U.S. App Store. This Privacy Policy is governed by U.S. law.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version at https://defdamian.github.io/tappaloo/privacy.html and change the effective date above. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will provide notice in the App or in the App Store release notes. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.
