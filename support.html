Frequently asked questions

Is Tappaloo free?
Yes. Tappaloo is completely free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Do I need an account or internet?
No. There are no accounts, and every game works offline.

How do I change my name?
Tap your avatar at the top of the Play screen, or go to Settings → Profile.

Where are my scores stored? Can I move them to a new phone?
Your scores, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. If you restore your new device from an iCloud or computer backup, they come with it. We never receive or store your data, so we can't recover it for you.

How do I reset my statistics?
Go to Settings → Statistics Data. You can reset one game or everything.

How do I hide games I don't like?
Go to Settings → Manage Games and switch off any game.

How do I turn reminders on or off?
Go to Settings → Reminders. You can also manage notifications in the iOS Settings app.

Why did I lose 2 seconds?
Most timed games take 2 seconds off the clock for a mistake. Each game's rules are on its start screen ("How to play").

Is Tappaloo available outside the U.S.?
Tappaloo is currently offered only in the United States.

Is there real gambling in the card games?
No. Card games are for entertainment only. There's no money, no wagering and no prizes.

Health & safety

Some games have flashing colors and fast movement. If you have a history of seizures or epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing. Take regular breaks, and never play while driving or walking in traffic.
