Frequently asked questions

Is Tappaloo free?
Yes. Tappaloo is completely free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Do I need an account or internet?
No. There are no accounts, and every game works offline.

How do I change my name?
Tap your avatar at the top of the Play screen, or go to Settings → Profile.

Where are my scores stored? Can I move them to a new phone?
Your scores, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. If you restore your new device from an iCloud or computer backup, they come with it. We never receive or store your data, so we can't recover it for you.

How do I reset my statistics?
Go to Settings → Statistics Data. You can reset one game or everything.

How do I hide games I don't like?
Go to Settings → Manage Games and switch off any game.

How do I turn reminders on or off?
Go to Settings → Reminders. You can also manage notifications in the iOS Settings app.

Why did I lose 2 seconds?
Most timed games take 2 seconds off the clock for a mistake. Each game's rules are on its start screen ("How to play").

Is Tappaloo available outside the U.S.?
Tappaloo is currently offered only in the United States.

What are stars for? Can I buy them?
Stars are earned by playing, beating the Daily Challenge, finishing weekly missions and unlocking trophies. Spend them in the Reward Shop (Rewards tab) on app colors, app icons and hats for Tappy. Stars can't be bought and have no real-money value.

What is the Daily Challenge?
Every day everyone gets the same game and target score. Beat it for 10 stars and share your result with friends.

What are Easy, Hard and Zen mode?
Pick them on any game's start screen. Easy gives more time and smaller penalties, Hard gives less time and bigger penalties, and Zen has no timer at all (Zen rounds don't count toward best scores).

How do I play with a friend?
In Four in a Row and Hearts & Stars, turn on "2 players on this device" on the start screen and take turns.

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Tappaloo, and choose Streak or Daily Challenge. Lock Screen widgets are also available.

Can I use Siri?
Yes. Say "Play the daily challenge in Tappaloo" or "Play Snake in Tappaloo".

How do I turn on Game Center or iCloud sync?
Go to Settings → Game Center & iCloud. Both are off by default.

Where are my Color by Math pictures?
In the Stats tab → Art Gallery. Tap a picture to share it or save it to Photos.

Can I turn off the music?
Yes: Settings → Sound & Haptics. You can also adjust the volume of the music and the sound effects separately.

Is the app in Spanish?
Yes. If your iPhone or iPad is set to Spanish, Tappaloo's menus and game instructions appear in Spanish. Word games (Word Scramble, Word Search, Mini Crossword) use English words.

Is there real gambling in the card games?
No. Card games are for entertainment only. There's no money, no wagering and no prizes.

Health & safety

Some games have flashing colors and fast movement. If you have a history of seizures or epilepsy, talk to a doctor before playing. Take regular breaks, and never play while driving or walking in traffic.
